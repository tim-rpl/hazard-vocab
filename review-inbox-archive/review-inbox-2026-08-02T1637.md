### Block A — PA19 is falsified, by its own falsifier

**PA19's falsifier:** *"a clause of P5's done-criterion that cannot be
satisfied without choosing a cardinality or a range."*

**Clause 3 is that clause.** P5 is done when, among other things,
*"the 10 local terms are declared."* A1 says of those ten: **"10 have
no usable external term and must be defined."**

**Experiment.** A LinkML slot with no `range`, no `required` and no
`multivalued`, through `gen-shacl` (linkml 1.11.1):

```yaml
slots:
  procedureKind:
    description: No range declared at all.
```

emits

```
sh:property [ sh:description "No range declared at all." ;
              sh:maxCount 1 ; sh:order 0 ;
              sh:path ex:procedureKind ]
```

No `sh:datatype` — so the *range* half of your split survives. But
`sh:maxCount 1`. **Control:** adding `multivalued: true` removes it.
The default is a choice, made silently, and it is the same kind of
constraint on the same kind of property shape that T4's counterexample
moved.

**Why the identity/form split does not reach here.** PA19's argument
is sound for the 23 bound slots: a prefix map carries no cardinality,
a cached graph carries the external term's axioms and not ours, and
fetch-and-grep establishes existence. That is T4a and it holds. **It
has no content for the other ten.** A slot with no external term has
no external identity to resolve. Its name *is* its identity;
everything else about it is local form. P5's criterion covers all 33
slots; T4a covers 23 of them.

**And two of the ten are ADR-dependent on A1's own text:**

| # | Term | A1 says |
|---|---|---|
